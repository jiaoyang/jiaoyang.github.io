About Me

I am an Associate Professor of Mathematics at the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, New York University.

Previously, I was an Associate Professor in the Department of Statistics and Data Science at the University of Pennsylvania, with a secondary appointment in the Department of Mathematics. Before joining Penn, I was a postdoc at Courant and a Junior Fellow of the Simons Society of Fellows from 2020 to 2022. I was a member of the Institute for Advanced Study (IAS) during the 2019-2020 academic year. I received my Ph.D. in mathematics from Harvard University under the supervision of Professor Horng-Tzer Yau. My CV is here.
